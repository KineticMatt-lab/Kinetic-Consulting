We help Asset Managers modernize their data architecture and investment book of record (IBOR).

Whether you're migrating from a legacy system, implementing a new front-to-back platform, or simply trying to get clean, real-time data across risk, performance, and reporting teams — we deliver production-ready solutions fast.

Typical Engagements

  • Full IBOR transformation and data governance framework
  • SimCorp Dimension, BlackRock Aladdin, or Eagle PACE implementations & optimizations
  • Golden source data strategy and reconciliation automation
  • Risk and performance alignment (attribution, stress testing, regulatory reporting)
  • Cloud migration and modern data platform architecture (Snowflake, Databricks, AWS)

Results You Can Expect

  • Single source of truth for positions, cash, and transactions
  • 60-70% reduction in manual data processes
  • Audit-ready documentation and controls
  • Faster month-end and client reporting
