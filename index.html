Investment firms face increasingly complex data landscapes — fragmented systems, legacy IBORs, and platforms that don't talk to each other. The result: manual workarounds, delayed reporting, and risk blind spots.

Kinetic Consulting brings senior-level expertise directly to your team. We don't hand off to junior staff. Every engagement is run by practitioners who've sat in your seat and delivered real transformations at firms like yours.

We work across the full front-to-back stack — from data strategy and architecture through platform implementation, reconciliation automation, and regulatory reporting — and we stay until it's in production.
